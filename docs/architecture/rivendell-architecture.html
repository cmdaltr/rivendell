Sets out from the Shire
Gandalf acquisition
gandalf.sh and gandalf.py (Linux, macOS; local or over SSH) and Invoke-Gandalf.ps1 (Windows, as admin). Locked files come straight off the NTFS volume with Gandalf's own reader. Every file is SHA-256 hashed into log.audit; the archive is encrypted, and in Key mode the key rides home as <host>.shadowfax.key.

Delving in Moria
Elrond worker Celery
Jobs arrive through Redis. The worker runs Elrond, and Elrond runs its tools as separate processes: Volatility 3, Plaso, Artemis, RegRipper, YARA, The Sleuth Kit.

The King in Minas Tirith
Aragorn administration
Andúril: Rivendell's own containers (status, logs, restart; won't stop the worker mid-job). The lab: Linux targets, Tart and UTM VMs, test runs from the browser, Gandalf against seeded hosts, Mordor recordings.
Where the shadows lie
Mordor attack datasets
OTRF Security-Datasets (APT29 day 1 and 2, Atomic sets) and the lab's own recordings of Atomic Red Team tests. --Mordor ingests them as event logs, so detections and ATT&CK tagging can be checked against known attacks.
